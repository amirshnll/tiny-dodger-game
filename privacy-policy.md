# Privacy Policy for Tiny Dodger

Tiny Dodger does not collect personal information, browsing history, website content, location data, or advertising identifiers. The game runs within the extension popup without advertising, analytics, or tracking. No data is sent to a server, sold, or shared with third parties.

## Data Stored

The extension stores language, sound, screen-shake, reduced-motion, and control preferences, together with the best score, longest survival time, and number of games played. This data is used only to preserve the user's settings and personal game records.

## Why the `storage` Permission Is Required

The `storage` permission is used only to keep game settings and records after the extension is closed. It does not allow access to personal files, browsing history, tabs, or website content, and it is not used to transmit information over the internet.

## External Link

The donation link opens an external website only when selected by the user. The privacy policy of that website applies after the user leaves the extension.
